About UnixTime

A lightweight, independent Unix timestamp converter.

What this site does

UnixTime converts Unix seconds, milliseconds, microseconds and nanoseconds to human-readable dates and back. The clock uses your device's clock, and conversion works locally in JavaScript. No conversion API or login is needed.

Accuracy and limitations

Unix time measures elapsed seconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC, excluding leap seconds. For microseconds and nanoseconds, calculations preserve integer precision, but readable JavaScript Date objects have millisecond resolution. Local timezone display uses the timezone data available in your browser. When a timestamp's unit is ambiguous, select it manually; do not rely on digit-length detection for historical values.

This is an independent educational utility, not affiliated with the developers of EpochConverter or any standards organization. For critical systems, validate results against your trusted reference implementation.

Privacy and advertising

The converter operates entirely within your browser. Read the privacy policy. No advertising networks or analytics trackers are installed in this initial release; future monetization requires an updated notice and applicable consent controls.
