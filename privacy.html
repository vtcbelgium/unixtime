Privacy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Conversion data

Your timestamps and dates are processed on your device using JavaScript. UnixTime does not transmit conversion inputs to a server. A timestamp can be included in a shareable link only when you use “Copy link”; do not share timestamps that contain sensitive information.

Website hosting

The hosting provider may process technical request logs, including IP addresses, user agents and network metadata necessary to serve and protect the site. The hosting provider and any external services used will be identified when deployment is finalized.

Analytics and advertising

No advertising scripts or analytics trackers are included in this initial build. If advertising or analytics is added, this policy will be updated and appropriate consent controls implemented where required before collecting or sharing visitor data.

Local storage

UnixTime does not write conversion data to cookies or local storage in this version.
